# Two Lines to Your Past Self

A two-line warning sent back to one specific moment: on 14 March 2019, miss the last train home from Manchester on purpose — because the woman waiting beside you becomes your wife, and your daughter only exists because you stayed. The program writes exactly those two lines to `/output/entry.txt`: line one names the moment, line two traces what changed because of it.
